How to Play

Object of the Game

  • Play each hole in the fewest throws (strokes) possible.
  • The player with the lowest total strokes for the course wins.
  • Safety first — never throw when players or park users are within range.
  • Always give park users the right of way.

Guidelines for Play

  • Each hole begins at the tee pad.
  • Subsequent throws are made from where the disc comes to rest.
  • Whoever is furthest from the basket throws first.
  • A hole ends when the disc is in the basket.
  • Lowest score on the previous hole tees off first.

Rules of Play

  • Keep a supporting point behind the disc, in line with the target, on release.
  • Within 30 feet of the basket, hold your lie until the disc is at rest.
  • Take casual relief from an unsafe lie.

Courtesy & Etiquette

  • Respect the course — no littering, no altering trees or obstacles.
  • Stay quiet and avoid distracting others mid-throw.
  • Stand behind the thrower until the throw is complete.
  • Let faster groups play through when possible.
